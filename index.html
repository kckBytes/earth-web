    How to use

    • Double-tap the globe to zoom in there; again at full zoom to come back out.
    • Drag to turn the Earth, pinch to zoom.
    • ● tap: next city · hold and move: fly anywhere.
    • ◷ hold: fast-forward time (watch day and night sweep and the clouds stream).
    On iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to get Earth as an app, full screen.

    Real sunlight, city lights and live clouds from five weather satellites (GOES-East/West, Himawari, Meteosat), stitched every 10 minutes (earth-live) and moved along the measured winds. Contains modified EUMETSAT data. Imagery: NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble. Places: Open-Meteo.
